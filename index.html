How to Calculate E-commerce ROAS

Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) is the heartbeat metric for your digital marketing. It measures the revenue generated for every dollar spent on advertising campaigns like Meta, TikTok, or Google Ads.

The ROAS Formula

The standard ROAS formula is simple: Total Revenue from Ads ÷ Total Ad Spend = ROAS. For example, if you spend $150 on ads and generate $450 in sales, your ROAS is 3.0x (or 300%).

What is a Good ROAS for E-commerce?

A "good" ROAS depends entirely on your profit margins. While a 4:1 ROAS is generally considered a solid benchmark in e-commerce, stores with low product costs (high margins) might be highly profitable at a 2.5x ROAS, whereas stores with thin margins might need a 4.5x ROAS just to break even.

Why Profit Margin Matters More

You cannot eat ROAS; you eat profit. That's why this calculator combines your Product Cost with your ad spend. By knowing your Net Profit, you can determine your exact Break-Even ROAS and scale your ad budgets without losing money.
